---
title: Programs
description: Browse incentive programs, their requirements, and incentive tiers.
---

<Info>
  **Base URL:** `https://api.leap.energy` (production) · `https://api.staging.leap.energy` (staging)

  **Authentication:** send your Leap API key as a Bearer token —
  `Authorization: Bearer <api-key>`.
</Info>

Programs describe which incentives exist, the requirements a customer must meet
to qualify, and the payout tiers. These endpoints are read-only for partners —
use them to discover what you can look up eligibility against.

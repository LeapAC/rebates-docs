description: "Surface energy incentives at the point of sale and claim them on your customers' behalf"
---

Leap Incentives lets you use energy incentives to drive sales of your product. Embed them directly into your point of sale and customer experience: instead of navigating incentive programs on their own, customers see what they qualify for and apply right from your product.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check eligibility">
    Send the customer's address and devices. Leap returns the programs they qualify for, the total incentive, and a Connect URL the customer can use to apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim the incentive">
    The customer completes their application in Leap Connect while you supply the data you already have on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress and payments">
    Follow each application through processing and, for partner offers, track payouts to your customers.
  </Step>
</Steps>
